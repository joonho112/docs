Who Leaves? Experience, Credentials, and Early-Career Risk in the Pre-K Workforce

Turnover differences among lead and auxiliary teachers, 2021–22 to 2025–26
JoonHo Lee and Alison Hooper · August 2026

Abstract

The companion brief in this series showed that about one in five First Class Pre-K teachers leaves the program each year and that half of a cohort is gone within three, and we examine here who those leavers are. Using the same 11,651 teacher-year transitions from the program’s rosters (2021–22 through 2025–26), we break turnover down by experience, credential, role, and site size. Departures are not spread evenly across the workforce. They concentrate at the start of careers, where more than one in four teachers with two or fewer years of experience leaves the program in a given year, and among teachers working under credential waivers, whose exit rate is roughly double that of teachers with degrees.
