Access 2026: Enrollment, Waitlists, and Where the Pressure Is

County enrollment and waitlist patterns (2024–25) and the first site-level look at sign-ups (November 2025)
JoonHo Lee and Alison Hooper · August 2026

Abstract

This brief updates the series’ access baseline with First Class Pre-K’s 2024–25 county counts, and it adds the first analysis of the program’s site-by-site waitlist sign-ups. In the county data, 24,238 of Alabama’s 59,016 four-year-olds, or 41.1 percent, were enrolled and 5,243, or 8.9 percent, were waitlisted, while model-adjusted county enrollment shares run from about 19 to 93 percent. The site file changes how we read the waitlist itself: as of November 2025, its 7,523 site sign-ups traced back to 4,679 distinct children, because three in ten waitlisted children are signed up at more than one site. Although both sources describe access, they cover different program years, and we keep them in separate sections throughout.
