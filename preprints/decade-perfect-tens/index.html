A Decade of Perfect Tens: Where Does Alabama Pre-K Stand in the Nation?

Quality benchmarks, enrollment, and state spending across 51 jurisdictions, 2016–2025
JoonHo Lee and Alison Hooper · August 2026

Abstract

Alabama’s First Class Pre-K is routinely described as one of the highest-quality state pre-K programs in the country. In this brief we examine what that reputation looks like in the national data, comparing Alabama with the other 50 states and the District of Columbia on quality standards, enrollment, and state spending. We assembled a 51jurisdiction panel from the National Institute for Early Education Research (NIEER) State of Preschool yearbooks and focus on the 2025 edition (the 2024–25 school year). On quality standards we find Alabama’s record singular: it is the only state to have met all 10 of NIEER’s current quality benchmarks in every year since the framework was adopted in 2016. On access we observe a different picture, as 40.3 percent of Alabama’s four-year-olds are enrolled, 18th among the 51 jurisdictions.
