Same Program, Different Paychecks: Pay, Benefits, and the Parity Gap

Lead-teacher compensation across delivery settings, 2021–22 to 2024–25
JoonHo Lee and Alison Hooper · August 2026

Abstract

Every First Class Pre-K classroom operates under the same quality standards and the same state salary schedule, and we examine here whether the teachers in those classrooms are compensated in the same way. We linked 5,753 lead-teacher salary records from the program’s rosters (2021–22 through 2024–25) to the state minimum salary schedule, and we drew on the program’s classroom budget data for benefits. Our analysis shows that public-school teachers are paid, on average, $3,584 above their schedule amount, while Head Start teachers sit $4,398 below it and other non-public teachers $5,623 below. The difference in benefits is wider still, at $20,548 a year in budgeted lead-teacher benefits in public schools versus $3,617—about $300 a month—in other non-public settings.
