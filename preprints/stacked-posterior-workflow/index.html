One Markov Chain Monte Carlo Fit for Many Plausible Values: A Calibrated Stacked Posterior Workflow for Bayesian Multilevel Models of Large-Scale Assessment Data

JoonHo Lee, Matthew R. Williams, and Terrance D. Savitsky · August 2026

Abstract

Large-scale assessments such as the Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA) report each student’s proficiency as multiple plausible values (PVs), latent traits published as draws from a marginal posterior distribution. The PVs, in turn, are analyzed by data users through fitting a secondary model once per PV and pooling the results with the combining rules of multiple imputation. For a Bayesian multilevel model, this convention requires one Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) run per plausible value. We develop a workflow in which a single MCMC run suffices. All plausible values enter one stacked fit, and the reported estimate and total variance are assembled from deterministic per-PV fits. This paper formulates an exact affine correction and calibrates the fixed-effect posterior draws to the assembled target. A simulation across multiple conditions shows accurate estimation and near-nominal interval coverage. In an application to PISA 2022 reading data for the United States and Korea, the recommended workflow reproduces the per-PV estimates and standard errors. The open-source R package pvstackr implements the workflow.
