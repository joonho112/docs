Who Stays? Four Years of Teacher Turnover in First Class Pre-K

Annual and cumulative turnover among lead and auxiliary teachers, 2021–22 to 2025–26
JoonHo Lee and Alison Hooper · August 2026

Abstract

Every fall, First Class Pre-K classrooms open with a mix of familiar and new faces, and we examine here how many of the previous year’s teachers returned to the program’s rosters. Because those rosters carry no stable staff identifier, we linked records across five school years, 2021–22 through 2025–26, resolving 14,674 roster records into roughly 5,200 individual teachers and 11,651 year-to-year transitions. Our analysis shows that in a typical year about one in five teachers is absent from the program’s rosters entirely, and about one in four is no longer at the same site. Following the teachers on a single year’s rosters, we observe that half of them are gone within three years.
